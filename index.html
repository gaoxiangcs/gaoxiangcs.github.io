Research & Engineering

APR 2025 — PRESENT · BRAIN-TO-AUDIO

MEG-to-Audio Representation Decoding

Developed and evaluated a first-stage decoding pipeline using Forrest Gump viewing data from 11 participants and 272 shared MEG channels, including preprocessing and synchronized audio targets.

Implemented 22 subject-specific ridge-regression baselines and evaluated a shared CNN–Transformer with CLAP, BEATs, and acoustic targets. Held-out tests revealed a trade-off between clip retrieval and acoustic reconstruction. Ongoing research.

JUL — OCT 2026 · RESEARCH CONTRIBUTOR & PAPER CO-AUTHOR

AAArena: Benchmarking LLM Agents in Adversarial Games

Reconstructed the DOTO and Miracle editions of the Tsinghua University AI Agent Competition as reusable benchmark tasks for code-writing agents. Contributed competition resources and evaluation setup for these two editions within the 12-game AAArena benchmark.

2026 · MEDICAL–ENGINEERING PROJECT

Motor Imagery–Driven Closed-Loop Functional Electrical Stimulation

Developed a motor-intent recognition model and integrated an EEG headset with a functional electrical stimulation (FES) subsystem into an end-to-end prototype for spinal cord injury rehabilitation research.
